Support

Need help with PocketFold? Write to turker.alperenn@gmail.com. Please include your device model, your iOS version and a short description of what happened. We aim to reply within a few days.

Common questions

The foldable layout does not appear on my iPhone Duo.

Foldable layouts need iOS 27.1 or later. Update the app and iOS, then open Settings › Layout inside PocketFold. You can force Clamshell or Wide there if the console is not detecting the hinge.

How do I go back to the game library?

Hold Start + Select together in any game. You can also use the system menu on the console.

Closing the lid paused my game. Is my progress saved?

Yes. Closing the lid, leaving the app and quitting all pause the game and save your progress automatically.

Can I use a game controller or keyboard?

Yes. PocketFold supports game controllers and keyboards in addition to the on-screen D-pad and buttons.

I have no sound, or the haptics are too strong.

Check your silent switch and volume. Inside PocketFold open Settings to change music and effects volume, mute, and turn haptics off.

How do I erase my save data?

Open Settings › Save data on the console and choose erase. The app asks you to confirm.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in and choose the purchase.

Is any of my data collected?

No. See the Privacy Policy.
