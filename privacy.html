Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

PocketFold ("the app") is a game by Alperen Türker ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. The short version: we do not collect any data from you.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs that send data anywhere. It does not need an internet connection to work.

What is stored on your device

  • Your game progress and settings (for example volume, layout and console finish). These are saved in a local file inside the app's storage on your device.
  • This data never leaves your device through the app. If you back up your device with iCloud or your computer, Apple may include the app's local data in that backup under Apple's own terms.
  • Deleting the app removes this data. You can also erase game data from Settings inside the app.

Device features the app uses

  • Foldable display state: on iPhone Duo the app reads the hinge angle and the fold position so it can lay out the console. This is used on the device only and is not stored or sent.
  • Haptics and audio: used for button feedback and sound. The app does not use the microphone, camera, location, contacts or photos.

Third parties

We do not share data with third parties because we do not collect any. Apple processes your App Store purchase under Apple's own privacy policy.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. It contains no chat, no user-generated content and no links out to the web.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you are in the EEA, UK or Türkiye (GDPR, UK GDPR, KVKK), you can still contact us with any question about this policy.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes how it handles data, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date before the change ships, and update the App Store privacy details.
