Good to know

Do I need an iPhone Duo?

No. PocketFold also runs on regular iPhones. The foldable layouts need an iPhone Duo with iOS 27.1 or later.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

No. You pay once and everything is included.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. PocketFold works fully offline and does not collect data.
